# Twin City Fence — 90-day plan

Written 2026-09-21. Revisit at day 30.

## The honest starting position

**There is no baseline yet, and that is the first finding rather than a gap in
the plan.**

The only Search Console property that existed was
`http://www.twincityfences.com/` — a URL-prefix property on the wrong protocol
*and* the wrong host, left over from the Weebly site. It read 0 clicks over
three months, which was never "this business gets no search traffic"; it was
Search Console watching a hostname that stopped existing.

A domain property now exists and covers everything. It reads zero because it
is hours old.

So the first job is not to improve anything. It is to find out what is
actually there. Any plan that commits to a ranking target before day 30 would
be inventing its own starting line.

For contrast, 24 Hour Towing Dallas — whose property already existed —
returned 579 clicks and 421,505 impressions the moment it was connected. That
is what a real baseline looks like, and Twin City may or may not resemble it.

## Days 1–14: find out what is true

- Let the domain property fill. Re-run `npm run gsc` at day 7 and day 14.
- Record the first real numbers: impressions, clicks, average position, which
  queries, which pages.
- **Answer the question that decides this whole plan**: does
  twincityfences.com have existing search visibility, or is this closer to a
  standing start? The answer changes what months two and three should be.
- Fix the four citation name errors (`docs/twin-city-fences-citations.md`).
  Cheap, entirely under our control, and worth doing while waiting for data.
- Confirm or drop Yelp's "over 20 years experience" claim.
- Confirm or remove the three services nothing independently evidences: farm
  and agricultural, gate installation, fence replacement.

**Nothing in this fortnight depends on the data arriving, which is the point.**

## Days 15–45: the things that are true regardless

- **Call tracking**, once the number is ported. Until then every phone lead is
  invisible and the conversion picture has a hole in it. This is the single
  biggest measurement gap left.
- **Watch the rebuilt pages.** Fifteen pages exist where there was one. Search
  Console will show which earn impressions and which do not — and on Dallas
  that check already overturned an assumption, so expect it to here.
- **Photography.** There is one hero image and no gallery. A fence contractor
  with no pictures of finished fences is asking a stranger to phone about a
  four-figure job on the strength of paragraph text.
  `docs/photo-shot-list.md` has the per-site list.
- **Fix whatever the weekly audit surfaces.** It runs Mondays.

## Days 46–90: only now, and only on evidence

What belongs here cannot be written honestly today, because it depends on what
days 1–14 reveal. Two branches:

**If there is existing visibility** — pages ranking 5–20 are the fastest gains
available. Improve those specific pages rather than writing new ones. This is
what Dallas needs: position 14.3 on 285,000 impressions is page two on real
volume, and moving it is worth more than any new content.

**If it is a standing start** — the work is local relevance and a reason to be
linked. Supplier and manufacturer "find an installer" pages, the Monroe
Chamber, and genuine local sponsorship. Slow, legitimate, and the only thing
that works from zero.

Either way, link prospecting starts here and not before. Drafted for approval,
never auto-sent.

## What this plan will not do

- **No citation-building package.** Six citations exist and four are wrong.
  Buying forty more spreads the error.
- **No blog.** For a fence contractor, service and service-area pages earn
  their keep; a blog usually does not.
- **No new city pages** unless the business genuinely works there and the page
  says something a Monroe page does not. Twelve near-identical city pages is
  the doorway pattern Google's scaled-content policy is written about.
- **No invented claims.** No warranties, no years in business, no response
  times, no review counts. That is what had to be stripped out of SeaCoast and
  it is cheaper to never write than to unpick.

## How to tell if it worked

At day 90, in order of how much they matter:

1. Phone calls, attributed — needs call tracking, hence its priority.
2. Form leads. The form is live and delivering; today it is one test lead.
3. Impressions and clicks on the fifteen pages, against the day-14 baseline.
4. Average position on the queries the business actually wants.
5. Six citations in agreement.

**Rankings are fourth on that list on purpose.** The business needs the phone
to ring. A position improvement that produces no calls is a number, not a
result.
