## Competitive picture

### Land clearing / forestry mulching — HARDER than it looks

Established operators with real history and equipment, all fighting for the same
Alexandria terms:

- Louisiana Hydro-Ax Services — trading since 2005
- Louisiana Forestry Solutions — since 1991, Many LA, covers Alexandria
- Louisiana Land Clearing — statewide, multiple machine sizes
- Southern Land Dynamics — dedicated Alexandria location page
- Brelands Land Clearing & Forestry Mulching — Alexandria 71301, the only one
  that is actually local

**The opening:** almost all of these are *statewide* operators running programmatic
city pages from a base that is not Alexandria. They compete organically. They
cannot compete on proximity in the local pack. A genuine Deville GBP can.

### Mobile-home demolition / removal — MUCH SOFTER, and this is the wedge

The first page is mostly directories and out-of-state doorway pages:

- texasmobilehomedemolition.com — a Texas site with a thin Alexandria page
- buzzmobilehomedemolitionservice.com — doorway
- hometowndemolitioncontractors.com — aggregator/directory
- localmobilehomebuyers.com — lead gen, not a contractor

Real local competition is thin: Bayou Rapides Corporation (2003), Site Work
Specialist LLC, Carlisle Charles LLC, DCTW (primarily tree removal).

**Reference project value:** mechanical mobile-home demolition is reported
around $4,000–$5,000 depending on size and location. Not our number and not to be
published as ours — it is here to support the high-ticket thesis.

## Why the GBP category matters more than any page

Primary category is the strongest relevance signal in the local pack. While the
profile's primary category is garbage or junk related, the site can be perfect and
the profile still will not surface for "land clearing" or "demolition". The owner
has added Demolition and plans to promote it to primary — **that single change is
worth more to map visibility than the entire page build.**

The rebrand to "Martins Trash Removal and Demolition" fixes the other half: the
name no longer contradicts the service.
