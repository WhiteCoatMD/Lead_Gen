# Fence permit guides: open questions for Mitch

Research date: 2026-09-23. Each bullet covers a place where the official text is
ambiguous, conflicting, possibly out of date, or silent. The guides in
`deploy/permits/*.json` state only the plainest safe reading, or "This isn't
published online", and none of these points is presented there as settled.
Please decide each one, usually with a phone call to the office named, before
the guide wording is tightened.

Municode links below use `https://library.municode.com/la/<place>/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=<node>`.

## Monroe (City)

- **Front-yard height: two rules overlap.** § 37-76(f)(4)a.1 allows solid fences up to 36" and 50%-open fences up to 42" "along the front yard boundaries". § 37-76(f)(4)a.2 says no chain-link fence "in any required front or side corner yard" may exceed 5 feet. It isn't clear whether a 5-ft chain-link fence is allowed in a front yard or only in a side-corner yard. The guide gives both rules and tells readers to ask. Source: [§ 37-76](https://library.municode.com/la/monroe/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=COOR_CH37ZO_ARTVSIDEST_S37-76ACSTUS)
- **What is allowed inside the corner "line of sight clearance area".** § 37-76(f)(4)a.3 allows 8-ft side/rear fences "except in the line of sight clearance area" (defined in § 37-21 as a 20-ft triangle along both property lines). Nothing says what height, if any, is allowed inside it. Separately, the nuisance code (§ 24.5-11(a)) defines a *35-ft* "sight-distance triangle" measured from the curb lines and treats obstructions there as nuisances. The guide mentions only the zoning triangle. Sources: [§ 37-21](https://library.municode.com/la/monroe/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=COOR_CH37ZO_ARTIIDERUIN_S37-21DE), [§ 24.5-11](https://library.municode.com/la/monroe/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=COOR_CH24.5NUBLNUBLAB_ARTIINUCL_S24.5-11NUOFPESA)
- **Office contact details come from two pages.** The Planning & Zoning phone number (318) 329-2334 comes from the [city directory](https://monroela.us/city-directory/). The address (400 Lea Joyner Expy, City Hall) is the site-wide footer on the [Planning & Zoning page](https://monroela.us/departments/engineering/planning-zoning/). Please confirm P&Z takes fence applications at City Hall.
- **Historic districts not covered.** Monroe has historic districts (Ch. 18.5), and its fee list includes a certificate of appropriateness, but I didn't check whether fences need one. Tell me if you want this added.

## West Monroe (City)

- **Front-yard 3-ft limit may apply only to sight-blocking fences.** § 12-5024(g)(1) limits to 3 ft any "fence, wall, hedge, structure or other object *which obstructs sight*" in a required front or side-corner yard, while (g)(2) allows chain link up to 5 ft there. It isn't clear whether open fences such as wrought iron may exceed 3 ft. The guide quotes both rules without deciding. Source: [§ 12-5024](https://library.municode.com/la/west_monroe/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PT12PLDE_CH5ZO_ARTCSURE_S12-5024SUYARE)
- **Two different corner visibility triangles.** § 12-5022(c) (residential corner lots) says nothing over 2 ft within 30 ft of the street-line intersection. The landscape regulations' definition in § 12-5091.2 says nothing over 3 ft within 20 ft of the right-of-way intersection (any district requiring a front yard). The guide states the stricter § 12-5022(c) rule and mentions the other. Sources: [§ 12-5022](https://library.municode.com/la/west_monroe/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PT12PLDE_CH5ZO_ARTCSURE_S12-5022SUARRE), [§ 12-5091.2](https://library.municode.com/la/west_monroe/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PT12PLDE_CH5ZO_ARTJLARE_S12-5091.2DE)
- **Phone number conflict.** The [Building & Development page](https://www.cityofwestmonroe.com/175/Building-Development) lists 318-396-2600. The city's [Building Permit Application PDF](https://www.cityofwestmonroe.com/DocumentCenter/View/1209/BUILDING-PERMIT-APPLICATION), which has a "FENCE" permit type, lists the Office of the Building Official at (318) 397-6722. The guide uses the web page number.
- **Two city websites.** westmonroe.la.gov still serves a Building & Zoning page, but it is stale (2018 content, "© 2013"). I used cityofwestmonroe.com, the site Municode lists for the city.
- **§ 5-1022 exception.** Fences in ditches and rights-of-way are prohibited "except as provided by section 5-1023". I didn't read § 5-1023, and the guide only mentions that it exists.

## Ouachita Parish (unincorporated)

- **Permit exemption may be out of date.** § 3½-123(b)(1)b exempts "fences not over six (6) feet high" from a building permit. That wording comes from the 2006 IRC, which § 3½-104 adopts. Louisiana's state uniform construction code has since moved to newer IRC editions. Municode also shows the parish code current only to **2015-02-06**, with 68 newer ordinances in OrdBank that I haven't checked. Please confirm the 6-ft threshold with the Permit Office, (318) 398-9780. Source: [§ 3½-123](https://library.municode.com/la/ouachita_parish/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=COOR_CH3_1-2BUST_ARTVBUCOPE_DIV2ADPR_S3_1-2_-123PE)
- **Parish zoning.** The permit office site has a "Planning and Zoning" page with no content, and I found no parish zoning ordinance on Municode that covers residential fences (height, corners, materials, pools). The guide says these aren't published online. [Permit Office](https://www.oppj.org/departments/permit_office/index.php)
- **Fee schedule correction.** The Ouachita Parish permit fee schedule PDF has no fence line. Only Monroe (§ 37-5) and West Monroe (§ 12-5024(g)(8)) state fence fees. Task-5-report.md said earlier that "the parish fee PDFs state fence fees"; that was wrong. The guides still state no fee amounts. [Fee schedule](https://www.oppj.org/Documents/Departments/Permit%20office/Ouachita%20Parish%20Permit%20Fee%20Schedule.pdf)
- **Two phone numbers on the permit office page.** The office block gives (318) 398-9780. The page sidebar gives (318) 327-1340, the Police Jury's main line. The guide uses 398-9780.

## Lake Charles (City)

- **Is any zoning sign-off needed for a fence?** § 6-40 exempts fences up to 10 ft from a *building* permit. The code doesn't say whether the Zoning Office reviews fences anyway. The guide says only what § 6-40 says. Source: [§ 6-40](https://library.municode.com/la/lake_charles/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIITHCO_CH6BUCOOCCORE_ARTIILOSTUNCOCO_S6-40PENORE)
- **Does the 10-ft cap apply everywhere, or only along arterial/collector corridors?** § 24-5-204(2)(b) reads: "No fence shall exceed ten feet in height or extend beyond the front setback line of the building along any arterial or collector roadway corridor." The guide (fact 3) reads the 10-ft cap as citywide and the front-setback limit as applying along those corridors. The sentence could also be read as limiting both to arterial/collector corridors. Please confirm with the Zoning Office (337-491-1542). Source: [§ 24-5-204](https://library.municode.com/la/lake_charles/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIITHCO_CH24ZO_ARTVDERE_PT2REGEAP_S24-5-204ACUS)
- **"Arterial or collector roadway corridor".** § 24-5-204(2)(b) bars fences beyond the building's front setback line along these corridors, but I found no list of which streets qualify. Source: [§ 24-5-204](https://library.municode.com/la/lake_charles/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIITHCO_CH24ZO_ARTVDERE_PT2REGEAP_S24-5-204ACUS)
- **Two sight-triangle rules.** § 24-5-203(2) sets a speed-based triangle (a table of leg distances, 40 ft at 4-way stops and signals, 15 ft along the controlled road). § 18-6 separately limits obstructions within 20 ft of a block corner to 36". The guide summarizes both without printing the speed table. Sources: [§ 24-5-203](https://library.municode.com/la/lake_charles/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIITHCO_CH24ZO_ARTVDERE_PT2REGEAP_S24-5-203DEST), [§ 18-6](https://library.municode.com/la/lake_charles/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIITHCO_CH18STSI_ARTIINGE_S18-6SAAXHEBUETPRWI20FEIN)
- **Pool barriers.** The city's swimming pool article (§§ 13-120, 13-121) covers only sanitation. Barrier rules may come from the adopted state construction code, which isn't published on Municode. The guide says this isn't published online.

## Sulphur (City)

- **Permit requirement is not in the code.** The city [FAQ](https://www.sulphurla.gov/m/faq) says to contact Inspections and Permits "to confirm permit requirements". The guide says exactly that and doesn't say yes or no.
- **Height.** No residential fence height limit appears in the code. Appendix B, Art. IV, Part 2, § 3(2)(d) says no accessory structure may exceed the height of the principal structure. § 3(2)(a) treats fences as a kind of accessory structure ("other than fences"). Whether that makes the house height the fence limit is unclear. The guide says this isn't published online. Source: [App. B Art. IV Part 2](https://library.municode.com/la/sulphur/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIICOOR_APXBLAUS_ARTIVDIES_PT2SPUS)
- **Cross-reference error.** § 3(2)(b) points to the corner clip at "subsection 2(2)", but the corner-clip text is in § 2(3). The guide uses the § 2(3) text (25 ft).
- **Utility setback wording.** § 17-39 (Ord. 2008, 1-12-26) bars structures including fences "within five (5) feet of a utility easement". It is unclear whether this means 5 ft beyond the easement's edge or something else. The guide repeats the wording. Source: [§ 17-39](https://library.municode.com/la/sulphur/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PTIICOOR_CH17STSIOTPUPL_ARTIIUTUSRI-WRE_S17-39RESECL)
- **Office address.** The [Permits & Inspections page](https://www.sulphurla.gov/196/Permits-Inspections) gives the Chief Building Inspector's phone (337-527-2050) and City Hall at 101 N Huntington St. Older sulphur.org PDFs place the Inspection Department at *110* N Huntington St. The guide uses the City Hall address from the current page.

## Westlake (City)

- **Code may be stale.** Municode shows the Westlake code last updated **2017-06-22**, with OrdBank enabled. Newer ordinances may change any of these rules. The city's own site links to this Municode code.
- **Where the fence rules sit.** The rules on fences along lot boundaries, one fence segment in a required side yard, and the 3-ft/30-ft corner triangle are printed as "supplementary regulations" inside § 12-2006(b) (Community unit plan). § 12-2005 applies § 12-2006 to every district, but the placement is odd. Please confirm with the city that they apply citywide. Source: [Ch. 2 Zoning](https://library.municode.com/la/westlake/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=PT12PLDE_CH2ZO)
- **"One (1) segment of a fence may be erected in a required side yard."** It's unclear what this allows or forbids. The guide repeats it word for word.
- **Permit.** The permit fact rests on § 12-2002 (fences are "structures") plus § 12-2008 (no structure erected without a building-inspector permit). The adopted building code's exemptions may say otherwise. Please confirm with the permit clerk at 337-433-0691.

## Calcasieu Parish (unincorporated)

- **Permit answer is only in a video.** The [Building Permits page](https://www.calcasieu.gov/services/planning-development/building-permits) answers "Do you need a permit to put up a fence?" in a YouTube video (o1vKiBrBDdY), which I couldn't read. The parish code adopts the **2006 IRC** (§ 6-241) and amends only item (1) of R105.2 (sheds up to 480 sq ft) in § 6-242. The IRC's own fence exemption isn't printed on Municode. The guide says the answer isn't in writing and gives the permit line, 337-721-3610.
- **Zoning coverage (CLOSED).** § 26-51 (fences along boundaries, 3-ft/30-ft corner triangle) is in the parish zoning chapter, and § 26-30 zones the whole unincorporated area, so § 26-51 applies to all homes outside city limits. Source: [§ 26-51](https://library.municode.com/la/calcasieu_parish_police_jury/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=COOR_CH26ZODE_ARTIIIZO_DIV6SCFE_S26-51FE)
- **Office address.** The page footer gives the Police Jury at 1015 Pithon St. with a P.O. Box ZIP of 70602. The Division of Planning and Development's walk-in location isn't stated. The guide uses the footer address.
- **Servitude rule is plat language.** § 26-13 is the dedication text required on subdivision plats. It may not bind lots in subdivisions platted before it, and the guide words it as plat language.

## All jurisdictions: Louisiana 811

- **2026 amendments.** Act 902 of 2026 (SB 469) amended parts of the damage prevention law (definitions renumbered, training exemptions, broadband projects). The 48-to-120-hour notice rule in R.S. 40:1749.13(B)(1) and the definition of excavation weren't changed. The guides quote the current text on legis.la.gov as read today.
- **Landowner exception not mentioned.** R.S. 40:1749.13(C) exempts owners digging up *their own underground utilities* on their own property. It doesn't cover fence posts, so the guides leave it out.
- **Pool barriers in the state code.** The Lake Charles, Westlake, Ouachita and Calcasieu guides now say pool barrier rules aren't printed in the local code of ordinances and that the adopted building code may set them, citing each place's adoption section. For those jurisdictions, the adopted state construction code may still have a pool barrier rule. Decide whether you want the guides to point to the state code.
